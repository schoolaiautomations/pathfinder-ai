CIRCLE 2

Subjects & skills

What to focus on in school, and which road to take after Class 10.

1Biology
You must know how drugs affect the human body and organs.
2Physical Science
Chemistry basics teach you how medicines are made and stored.
3Maths
You calculate doses, check quantities and manage stock every day.
After Class 10
Take BiPC (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) in Intermediate after Class 10. This is the required route for Pharmacy entrance. A Polytechnic Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) is a second route — cheaper, faster, leads to assistant pharmacist roles.

B.Pharm (Degree)

You must clear
Class 10 → BiPC Intermediate → EAPCET → B.Pharm or D.Pharm → licence registration → job
Do firstMany students skip practicals and fail drug calculation tests. Check early if you can handle chemistry lab work and are comfortable with maths.
CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

This is the circle almost nobody checks — and it decides most careers.

Who hires
Government hospitals (NHM, CGHS, AP Medical Services), private hospital chains, retail pharmacy shops, drug companies like Sun Pharma and Cipla, and self-owned medical shops.
How often
Openings come regularly but government posts are irregular — advertised every 1 to 3 years.
How many
Demand is steady but thousands of graduates compete for each government vacancy. Private retail jobs are easier to find.
CarefulBuild retail or hospital experience early so you are not waiting only for government jobs.
Your Plan B
Pick one that reuses the same preparation, so no year is wasted:

Ask at homeCan my family manage the years before I start earning? If you are the only earner-to-be, you need a plan that lets you earn while you prepare.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, starting today, not after results.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you are choosing this career for the wrong reasons. Talk to your counsellor.

Write a deadline today: “If I am not selected by the age of ___, I move to my Plan B.” The deadline is what protects you.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

If the four circles came out right, this is your road. Nothing here needs money or coaching.

1Right now, in school
  • Score above 75% in Biology, Chemistry and Maths consistently
  • Visit a nearby pharmacy and ask the pharmacist what their daily work looks like
  • Practice reading medicine strips and note the ingredients and doses
2After Class 10
  • Join BiPC stream in a college with a good chemistry lab
  • Attempt EAPCET mock tests from Year 1 of Intermediate
  • Apply for a D.Pharm seat as a backup option simultaneously
3After Intermediate or diploma
  • Register with the Andhra Pradesh Pharmacy Council after graduation
  • Take an internship in a hospital pharmacy before applying for jobs
  • Research opening your own licensed medical shop in your town as a long-term goal
RememberYou are in school. Nothing is decided yet — and that is your advantage. Every year you start early is a year you will not have to spend waiting later.

This is guidance, not an official notice. Qualifications, age limits, vacancies and pay change with every notification — always confirm on the official website before you apply, and never pay an agent for a seat.
