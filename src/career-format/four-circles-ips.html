CIRCLE 1

Do you really want it?

Many students like the uniform, status and action. The real job also includes paperwork, investigations, public complaints, court processes, night calls and responsibility when something goes wrong. Tap your honest answer.

Can you stay calm and make decisions during conflict, emergencies, crime scenes or tense public situations?

Are you willing to study law, criminal justice, investigation, public administration and current affairs — not just physical training?

Can you treat people fairly even when they are angry, accused of wrongdoing, politically influential or very different from you?

Can you handle transfers, night duty, emergency calls, public criticism and work that may sometimes involve personal risk?

Are you willing to maintain strong physical fitness and meet stricter medical/physical standards for the police service?

Would you still want this career if there were no social-media fame, sirens or visible authority — only responsibility for public safety and your team's actions?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & pathway

There is no separate “IPS course” after Class 10 or Class 12. IPS officers are selected through the UPSC Civil Services Examination after graduation.

1Social Studies
History, Geography, Civics/Polity and Economics become important for UPSC and for understanding government, society and law-and-order problems.
2English & Writing
UPSC requires reading, comprehension, essays and structured written answers. Police leadership also requires clear reports, briefings and communication.
3Fitness & Discipline
IPS has service-specific medical and physical requirements, and SVPNPA training includes demanding outdoor components. Build regular running, strength, mobility and healthy habits — not crash fitness just before selection.
After Class 10
Humanities / CEC is the most directly related route, but MPC, BiPC, Commerce or any recognised stream can lead to IPS. After Class 12, complete any recognised bachelor’s degree — BA, B.Com, B.Sc, B.Tech, MBBS, Law or another valid degree. UPSC does not require a specific graduation subject.

THE UPSC ROUTE — WHAT YOU WILL NEED LATER
Entrance exam
UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE). There is no separate IPS entrance exam. You become eligible for CSE after completing a recognised graduate degree. For IPS specifically, the candidate must also be an Indian citizen.
Prelims
Stage 1 — Civil Services Preliminary Examination. Two objective papers: General Studies Paper I and General Studies Paper II (CSAT). CSAT is qualifying at 33%. Prelims is only a screening stage; its marks are not added to the final merit score.
Mains
Stage 2 — Civil Services Main Examination. Nine descriptive papers, including qualifying language papers, Essay, four General Studies papers and two papers of one optional subject. It tests analysis, judgement and writing ability.
Interview
Stage 3 — Interview / Personality Test. Final ranking is based on the merit papers of Mains plus the Personality Test.
Medical & physical fitness
IPS has additional service-specific medical and physical standards. Candidates must meet the standards prescribed in the current Civil Services Examination medical rules. Do not rely on old internet charts for height, vision or other measurements — check the UPSC notification applicable in your exam year.
IPS allocation
Clearing UPSC does not automatically mean IPS. CSE recruits for IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS and many other services. Your final rank, category, service preferences, vacancies and medical eligibility determine whether you receive IPS.
You must clear
Class 12 in any stream → recognised bachelor’s degree → UPSC Prelims → UPSC Mains → Interview / Personality Test → IPS medical & physical fitness → final merit + service preference → IPS allocation → SVPNPA training → Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP).
ImportantIPS and Andhra Pradesh State Police recruitment are different routes. UPSC CSE is the direct-recruit route to IPS. Constable, Sub-Inspector and State Police Service / DSP-level recruitment are conducted through separate state recruitment systems.
CIRCLE 3 · THE COMPETITION

How difficult is selection?

IPS is a structured government career after selection. The major risk is getting selected and allocated to IPS in the first place.

Vacancies
UPSC's CSE 2026 expects about 933 vacancies across all participating Civil Services combined — not 933 IPS seats. The final IPS number for that examination can change later. As a recent official benchmark, CSE 2025 reported 150 IPS vacancies.
What this means
A very large number of graduates compete for a relatively small number of IPS allocations. You may clear stages of UPSC and still not receive IPS because service allocation depends on final merit, category, preferences and available seats.
Training reality
After selection, IPS probationers train at the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Police Academy (SVPNPA), Hyderabad. Training prepares officers for field leadership and includes criminal law, investigation, forensics, criminology, police management, ethics, human rights, internal security and ICT along with physical fitness, drill, weapon training, yoga, unarmed combat, field craft, tactics, map reading and equitation.
Job reality
IPS officers can work in district policing, crime investigation, intelligence, traffic, cybercrime, anti-corruption, special units, security, central police organisations and senior leadership posts. SVPNPA states that newly trained officers are posted initially as Assistant Superintendents of Police in their states/cadres.
CarefulThis is not an “action movie” career. A large part of good policing is documentation, investigation quality, evidence, personnel management, coordination, public communication, legal procedure and preventing problems before force is needed.

CIRCLE 4

What you will earn

IPS provides a strong government career and structured pay progression, but salary and authority are poor reasons to choose policing.

Starting basic pay
₹56,100/month basic pay
Direct-recruit IPS officers begin in the junior time scale / Level 10 pay structure. Applicable allowances and government facilities depend on rules and posting. Basic pay is not the same as monthly take-home.
Mid / senior levels
₹78,800 – ₹1,82,200/month basic
As officers move through senior police leadership levels, the official pay matrix rises with qualifying service and appointment to eligible posts.
Top levels
Up to about ₹2,25,000/month basic
Very senior IPS officers in the highest eligible pay levels can reach this basic-pay range. Only a small number of officers reach the top posts.
Preparation cost
UPSC's examination fee itself is small compared with the cost of coaching, accommodation and preparation time. You do not need expensive coaching from school. Good books, official syllabus, previous papers, disciplined self-study and answer practice matter more.
Ask yourselfWould you still choose IPS if another career paid more and gave you an easier lifestyle? If your main reasons are uniform, power, vehicle, security staff or social status, think again. Police leadership brings legal accountability, transfers, difficult hours, public scrutiny and real personal responsibility.

How to read your four circles

All four right — keep IPS as a serious goal while building a strong education, fitness and Plan B.

Any one wrong — stop and think carefully before committing years only to UPSC preparation.

Two or more wrong — you may like the uniform and image more than the work of policing. Talk to your counsellor.

One extra rule for IPS: authority is not the goal — lawful, fair and effective public safety is.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

An 8th–10th class student does not need UPSC coaching. Build knowledge, character, fitness and leadership first.

1Right now, in school
  • Understand Civics, History, Geography, Economics, Science and English instead of memorising only for marks
  • Build regular fitness: running, bodyweight strength, mobility and a healthy sleep routine
  • Take responsibility in sports, NCC/scouting if available, school clubs, volunteering or team activities — learn to lead without bullying people
2After Class 10
  • Choose Humanities / CEC if you genuinely like those subjects, but remember MPC, BiPC and Commerce can also lead to IPS
  • Improve current affairs, English/Telugu writing, public speaking, logical reasoning and basic understanding of law and the Constitution
  • Keep fitness consistent while completing Class 11–12; do not neglect academics for physical preparation
3During college & after graduation
  • Complete a recognised degree you value and build employable skills as your Plan B
  • Study the official UPSC syllabus and previous papers, then progress from NCERT/basic books to standard books, current affairs, answer writing and mock tests
  • Before listing IPS as a service preference, read the current UPSC medical/physical standards and understand the actual work, cadre system and training demands
Best first testImagine there is a serious fight near your school with rumours spreading online. Write one page answering: How would you protect people, verify what happened, prevent retaliation, collect evidence and treat both sides fairly? If calm, lawful problem-solving interests you more than “catching bad guys,” IPS may genuinely suit you.
