CIRCLE 1

Do you really want it?

Many students want the idea of the job, not the work. Tap your honest answer.

Can you stay calm when someone is bleeding or in serious pain?

Can you study for 6 or more hours every single day for years?

Are you okay seeing death, suffering, and failure up close regularly?

Can you follow instructions exactly, even when you disagree with them?

Are you fine skipping sleep, festivals, and holidays when patients need you?

Can you listen patiently to the same complaints from many patients daily?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & skills

What to focus on in school, and which road to take after Class 10.

1Biology
Everything in medicine — body parts, diseases, medicines — starts with biology.
2Physical Science
Physics and chemistry explain how medicines, equipment, and body systems work.
3English
Medical textbooks, exams, and patient notes are all written in English.
After Class 10
BiPC (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) after Class 10. This leads to NEET and MBBS admission. No real alternative route exists for becoming a doctor.

CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

This is the circle almost nobody checks — and it decides most careers.

Who hires
Government hospitals (AP Health Department, AIIMS, ESI), private hospitals (Apollo, KIMS, Yashoda), self-employment via own clinic.
How often
Openings come regularly but government seats are limited. Private practice is always possible.
How many
Over 20 lakh students write NEET yearly. Only about 1 lakh MBBS seats exist across India.
CarefulStart NEET preparation seriously by Class 9. Every mark matters.
Your Plan B
Pick one that reuses the same preparation, so no year is wasted:
PharmacistPhysiotherapistMedical lab technicianBiomedical researcherNurse or midwife

Ask at homeCan my family manage the years before I start earning? If you are the only earner-to-be, you need a plan that lets you earn while you prepare.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, starting today, not after results.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you are choosing this career for the wrong reasons. Talk to your counsellor.

Write a deadline today: “If I am not selected by the age of ___, I move to my Plan B.” The deadline is what protects you.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

If the four circles came out right, this is your road. Nothing here needs money or coaching.

1Right now, in school
  • Score above 90% in Biology and Physical Science every exam
  • Read one simple health or science article every week
  • Visit a government hospital with a family member to see real doctor work
2After Class 10
  • Join BiPC stream without any compromise or alternative
  • Start NEET coaching or use free YouTube resources like Unacademy or Khan Academy
  • Practice 50 NEET MCQs every single day from Class 11 itself
3After Intermediate or diploma
  • Write NEET-UG and apply to all government college options in AP and other states
  • If score is low, consider AYUSH or rewrite NEET once with full preparation
  • During MBBS, do every ward duty seriously — clinical skills matter more than marks
RememberYou are in school. Nothing is decided yet — and that is your advantage. Every year you start early is a year you will not have to spend waiting later.
