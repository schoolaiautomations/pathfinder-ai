CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

This is the circle almost nobody checks — and it decides most careers.

Who hires
Private IT companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Tech Mahindra), startups, product companies, freelance clients. Government IT jobs exist but are fewer. Hyderabad is the nearest big tech hub for AP students.
How often
Openings come every year, mostly April to August during campus hiring season.
How many
Lakhs of engineering graduates compete each year. Top colleges place better. Skills matter more than degree name.
CarefulLearn one coding language well before college. Skills beat marks in this field.
Your Plan B
Pick one that reuses the same preparation, so no year is wasted:
Data AnalystIT Support EngineerWeb DesignerMaths TeacherPolytechnic Lecturer in CS

And what it costs before the first salary arrives.

Senior (7+ years)
Rs 1,50,000 - 4,00,000
Leads teams or moves into architecture and management
The cost before that
B.Tech: 4 years, fees Rs 60,000 - 2,00,000 per year in government colleges, Rs 80,000 - 3,00,000 in private. Diploma: 3 years, much cheaper. Family funds roughly 3 to 4 non-earning years minimum.
Ask at homeCan my family manage the years before I start earning? If you are the only earner-to-be, you need a plan that lets you earn while you prepare.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, starting today, not after results.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you are choosing this career for the wrong reasons. Talk to your counsellor.

Write a deadline today: “If I am not selected by the age of ___, I move to my Plan B.” The deadline is what protects you.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

If the four circles came out right, this is your road. Nothing here needs money or coaching.

1Right now, in school
  • Practice typing daily on keybr.com — aim for 40 words per minute
  • Start free coding on Scratch or Code.org — build one small project
  • Score above 80 in Maths — fix weak chapters now, not later
2After Class 10
  • Join Intermediate MPC and focus hard on Maths and Physics
  • Appear for AP POLYCET if choosing diploma route — register on time
  • Learn basic Python free on YouTube for 30 minutes daily
3After Intermediate or diploma
  • Clear AP EAMCET and join a recognised B.Tech CSE college
  • Build 2 to 3 small projects and put them on GitHub before final year
  • Apply for internships in Year 3 — this is how most first jobs happen
RememberYou are in school. Nothing is decided yet — and that is your advantage. Every year you start early is a year you will not have to spend waiting later.

This is guidance, not an official notice. Qualifications, age limits, vacancies and pay change with every notification — always confirm on the official website before you apply, and never pay an agent for a seat.
