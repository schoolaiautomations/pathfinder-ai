Yoga & Naturopathy Doctor (BNYS)

A Yoga & Naturopathy professional with a BNYS — Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences degree works in naturopathy, therapeutic yoga, lifestyle counselling, diet planning and non-pharmacological wellness care within the scope permitted by the relevant registration and institutional rules. BNYS is a distinct professional pathway — it is not the same as MBBS.

CIRCLE 1

Would Yoga & Naturopathy suit you?

This career is more than knowing yoga poses. You study health sciences, examine patients, plan lifestyle-based care, supervise therapies, maintain records and work within a defined professional scope.

Do Biology, human health and how the body works interest you?

Would you enjoy teaching and supervising yoga or lifestyle changes for different people?

Can you listen patiently to a person's health history and explain recommendations clearly?

Would you be comfortable working in hospitals, wellness centres or residential naturopathy settings?

Can you follow evidence, safety rules and referral limits instead of claiming that one therapy can cure everything?

Are you willing to complete a long 5½-year professional course including internship?

CIRCLE 2

Which pathway can you take?

For Andhra Pradesh students, the direct route is simple: Class 10 → BiPC → BNYS admission → 5½-year BNYS including internship → registration as applicable → clinical/wellness career.

Course wordsBNYS = Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences. It is a professional undergraduate course in Naturopathy & Yoga. Dr. NTRUHS = Dr. N.T.R. University of Health Sciences, which currently conducts BNYS admissions in Andhra Pradesh.
After Class 10
Choose BiPC — Biology, Physics and Chemistry.

For the latest completed Andhra Pradesh BNYS admission cycle (2025–26), candidates needed Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English in Class 12 / Intermediate.
Minimum marks — AP example
In the 2025–26 Dr. NTRUHS prospectus, the minimum aggregate in Science subjects was 50% for OC, 40% for SC/ST/BC/PwD and 45% for OC-PwD, with English compulsory.

Always check the prospectus for your admission year because rules can change.
Entrance exam in Andhra Pradesh?
In the latest completed 2025–26 AP Competent Authority admission, BNYS merit was based on Intermediate marks in Biology, Physics and Chemistry.

The prospectus did not use NEET for this BNYS counselling cycle. Students applied through Dr. NTRUHS and seats were allotted through the university's merit/web-counselling process.

Do not assume this will remain unchanged forever — verify the current year's prospectus.
Course duration
BNYS = 5½ academic years, including 1 year compulsory rotatory internship under the current Dr. NTRUHS prospectus.

This is therefore a full professional healthcare course, not a short yoga certificate.
What you study
The curriculum combines basic health sciences and professional subjects such as Anatomy, Physiology, Pathology, Yoga, Nutrition/Dietetics, Naturopathy principles and clinical/practical training, with the exact syllabus prescribed by the university.
AP colleges — latest completed cycle
The 2025–26 Dr. NTRUHS Competent Authority prospectus listed:

CARE Yoga Naturopathy Medical College & Hospital, Bapatla
Sri Pathanjali Maharshi Naturopathy & Yoga Medical College, Guntakal

The Ministry of AYUSH's national institutional listing also includes these Andhra Pradesh BNYS colleges.
Registration after BNYS
Yoga & Naturopathy registration has historically been handled through state-level arrangements, with central registration initiatives also administered through the Yoga & Naturopathy system.

Before practising, graduates should verify the registration requirement that applies in the state where they intend to work.
Higher study
Depending on institution and state availability, BNYS graduates can pursue postgraduate study in Naturopathy, Yoga, Public Health, Hospital Administration, research and related areas. Teaching/research careers may value PG and PhD qualifications.
You must clear
Class 10 → BiPC → Class 12 with Physics + Chemistry + Biology + English → apply through the current BNYS admission process → BNYS 5½ years including 1-year internship → complete applicable registration → Naturopathy / Yoga clinical, wellness, teaching or research career.
Very importantBNYS is not MBBS. A BNYS graduate should work within the legally permitted scope of Naturopathy & Yoga practice and the rules of the state/institution. Do not assume a BNYS qualification automatically gives the same authority as an MBBS doctor to prescribe modern medicines, perform medical procedures or manage emergencies independently.
CIRCLE 3 · CAREER AREAS

What work can you do after BNYS?

BNYS careers can be clinical, wellness-focused, educational, research-oriented or entrepreneurial.

Naturopathy Consultant
Assess lifestyle, health history and wellness goals and provide naturopathy/lifestyle-based care within professional scope.
ConsultationLifestyleWellness
Yoga Therapy Practitioner
Use structured yoga practices such as movement, breathing and relaxation approaches adapted to the person's condition and abilities, within the practitioner's competence and institutional protocols.
Lifestyle & Diet Counselling
Support people with practical lifestyle routines, sleep, activity and diet planning within the boundaries of BNYS training and the clinical setting.
Hospital / Wellness Centre BNYS Doctor
Work with OP and IP clients/patients in naturopathy hospitals, AYUSH/integrative settings, wellness centres and residential health programmes.
Naturopathy Therapy Supervision
Supervise institutionally approved naturopathy modalities such as hydrotherapy, mud applications, massage and other non-pharmacological therapies taught within BNYS programmes.
Preventive / Lifestyle Programmes
Work in wellness, stress-management and lifestyle programmes focused on healthy routines and risk-factor reduction, while referring people for medical evaluation when needed.
Research
Work in Yoga & Naturopathy research through organisations such as the Central Council for Research in Yoga & Naturopathy (CCRYN), universities and research institutions. Advanced research roles often require postgraduate/PhD training.
Teaching
With the required experience and qualifications, BNYS graduates can move into teaching in Yoga & Naturopathy colleges. Government institutes may require experience and may prefer PG/PhD qualifications for senior academic roles.
Wellness / Retreat Industry
Work in health resorts, yoga retreats, corporate wellness programmes and integrative wellness centres. Communication and client-management skills are especially important here.
Own Clinic / Wellness Centre
After meeting applicable registration, local licensing and professional requirements, some graduates establish private Naturopathy/Yoga practices or wellness centres. Business success depends on trust, location, ethical practice and service quality.
BNYS vs MBBS
BNYS: Naturopathy & Yogic Sciences professional pathway focused on naturopathy, yoga and lifestyle-based approaches.

MBBS: modern-medicine medical degree covering diagnosis, pharmacology, surgery, emergency medicine and the full modern medical curriculum.

They are not interchangeable qualifications.
BNYS vs Yoga Instructor
BNYS professional: completes a 5½-year healthcare-oriented degree including clinical training and internship.
Yoga Instructor: may complete a certificate/diploma/degree focused mainly on teaching yoga.

A short yoga certificate is not equivalent to BNYS.
BNYS vs Physiotherapist
BNYS: naturopathy, yoga and lifestyle-health pathway.
Physiotherapist: trained specifically in physical rehabilitation, movement dysfunction and physiotherapy techniques through BPT and professional standards applicable to physiotherapy.
When referral matters
A responsible BNYS practitioner should recognise when a person needs conventional medical diagnosis, emergency treatment, surgery, medication management or specialist care. Lifestyle and yoga-based care should not delay necessary medical treatment.
Where you can work
Naturopathy hospitals, Yoga & Naturopathy medical colleges, integrative hospitals, wellness centres, resorts, rehabilitation/wellness programmes, research institutes, public-health projects and private practice subject to applicable rules.
Your Plan B
BNYS skills can also support:
Yoga TherapyWellness ManagementPublic HealthHospital AdministrationResearchTeaching
Professional realityGood Naturopathy practice should be safe, ethical and evidence-aware. Avoid promising cures for serious diseases, respect professional limits and refer appropriately when medical evaluation is needed.
CIRCLE 4

What you may earn

BNYS income varies widely between wellness centres, hospitals, teaching/research institutions and private practice.

Intern / trainee
Stipend varies by institution
BNYS includes a compulsory rotatory internship, but stipend arrangements differ by college/state/institution. Verify this before admission rather than assuming every internship is paid.
Starting private BNYS role
₹18,000 – ₹30,000/month
Current 2026 job postings include early-career BNYS / Naturopathy roles around this range, depending on city, employer and responsibilities.
India benchmark
≈ ₹28,969/month
Indeed's January 2026 India benchmark for Naturopath was about ₹28,969/month, based on only 7 reported salaries. Treat this as a rough reference, not a guaranteed BNYS salary.
Experienced wellness / consultant role
₹28,000 – ₹45,000+/month
Current 2026 BNYS job advertisements show some experienced/private wellness roles in this range. Premium wellness centres or management responsibilities can differ significantly.
Government / research institute example
Can be much higher with experience
National Institute of Naturopathy recruitment has previously listed Resident Medical Officer at Pay Level 10 and Assistant Professor at Pay Level 11, but these posts required BNYS plus substantial experience and/or additional qualifications. They are not fresher salary examples.
Own practice
Highly variable
Private practice income depends on location, patient volume, reputation, legal/registration requirements, operating costs and the services offered. Revenue is not the same as profit.
AP course-cost example
In the latest completed 2025–26 Dr. NTRUHS Competent Authority prospectus, the two listed AP private BNYS colleges had a government-notified tuition amount of ₹24,500 under the cited fee orders, in addition to university/other applicable charges. Fees can change, so always check the new prospectus.
Money warningDo not choose BNYS only because it contains the word “Doctor.” Compare the actual career market, scope of practice, registration rules, course duration and likely early-career income before committing 5½ years.

How to read your four circles

Health + yoga + lifestyle counselling — BNYS may be a strong fit.

Want modern medicine, drugs, surgery and emergency care — compare MBBS.

Love yoga but do not want a 5½-year healthcare degree — compare Yoga Instructor / Yoga Therapist education routes.

One extra rule: BNYS is a distinct professional health pathway — understand its real scope before choosing it simply because the graduate may work in a clinical setting.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

For an 8th–10th class student, test both sides of the career: the health-science side and the yoga/lifestyle side.

1Right now, in school
  • Strengthen Biology and basic Chemistry
  • Learn yoga from a qualified instructor and understand that safe teaching involves more than copying poses
  • Develop communication and listening skills because much of healthcare is understanding the person in front of you
2After Class 10
  • Choose BiPC
  • Complete Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English in Intermediate/Class 12
  • Check the current Dr. NTRUHS BNYS prospectus rather than assuming NEET or another exam is required
3During BNYS
  • Take Anatomy, Physiology, Pathology and clinical subjects seriously — not only Yoga
  • Use internship to understand real patient communication, documentation, safety and referral
  • Decide whether you prefer clinical practice, yoga therapy, wellness, teaching, research or public health
Best first testSpend a day observing a legitimate BNYS hospital or Yoga & Naturopathy medical college if permitted. Ask what practitioners actually do during OP consultations, inpatient care and internship. If the health-science + patient-care + lifestyle work interests you — not just yoga classes — BNYS may genuinely suit you.
