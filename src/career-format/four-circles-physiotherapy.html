Physiotherapy

Physiotherapists help people reduce pain, regain movement and return to daily life after injury, surgery, illness or disability. The career combines Biology, anatomy, exercise, hands-on assessment, rehabilitation and patient communication. It is not simply “massage” — it is a healthcare profession built around movement and function.

CIRCLE 1

Do you really want physiotherapy?

The work is very human. You may spend much of the day helping people who are in pain, weak, frustrated or afraid of moving again.

Do you enjoy Biology and understanding how muscles, joints, nerves, heart and lungs work?

Can you patiently encourage someone to repeat exercises even when progress is slow?

Are you comfortable working closely and professionally with patients of different ages and health conditions?

Would you enjoy learning anatomy, biomechanics, exercise therapy and clinical assessment rather than only sports and gym exercises?

Can you stay careful and evidence-based instead of promising a “quick cure” to every patient?

Would you still choose this career if your first salary were modest and you had to build experience before earning more?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & pathway

For students in Andhra Pradesh, the route is straightforward: BiPC is the safest school pathway into BPT.

1Biology
The strongest school foundation. Later you will study anatomy, physiology, pathology, neurology, exercise therapy and rehabilitation.
2Physics
Movement, force, balance, levers, gait and electro-physical modalities all depend on basic Physics and biomechanics.
3English & Communication
You must understand medical material, explain exercises clearly, take patient history and build trust. In Andhra Pradesh's current BPT eligibility, English is also a required Class 12 subject.
After Class 10
Intermediate BiPC — Biology, Physics and Chemistry — with English is the standard route. In the latest Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences Andhra Pradesh BPT admission rules, students needed Class 12 / Intermediate with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. After Class 12, apply for BPT admission under the current university/college process.
Entrance / admission options
There is no single compulsory national entrance exam for BPT across India.

CUET-UG — some universities across India admit BPT students through CUET-UG. If targeting a CUET-participating university, check that university's exact subject combination and BPT eligibility.

NEET-UG — may be accepted or required by some institutions/authorities, but it is not a compulsory BPT entrance exam across all of India. Always check the exact college/state rule.

University / state route — other universities or states may use their own entrance test, counselling process or Class 12 merit.
Andhra Pradesh
In the latest completed Dr. NTR UHS 2025–26 competent-authority admission cycle, BPT admission was based on Class 12 science marks rather than a separate BPT entrance exam. The merit list used the percentage of marks in Botany, Zoology, Physics and Chemistry, followed by web counselling.
Admission rules can change. Students applying in a later year should check that year's Dr. NTR UHS prospectus rather than assuming NEET, CUET or AP EAPCET is compulsory.
BPT course
Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT). Under Dr. NTR UHS's latest 2025–26 prospectus, the course is 4 academic years + 6 months compulsory rotatory internship — about 4½ years total.
What you study
AnatomyPhysiologyBiomechanicsExercise TherapyElectrotherapyOrthopaedicsNeurologyCardiorespiratory
Professional status
Physiotherapy is recognised under India's National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 2021. The Act specifically lists the Physiotherapy Professional / Physiotherapist and provides for professional standards and central/state registers. Because registration systems continue to evolve, verify the current registration requirement and recognised qualification when you graduate.
Higher study
MPT — Master of Physiotherapy can be used to specialise further. Dr. NTR UHS currently lists MPT specialities including Orthopaedics, Neurology, Cardiorespiratory and Sports. Higher study is useful for specialist clinical work, teaching and research, but is not the only way to begin working after BPT.
You must clear
Class 10 → Intermediate BiPC + English → BPT admission through CUET-UG / NEET-UG / university-state merit or entrance route as applicable → 4 years BPT + compulsory clinical internship → required professional registration / institutional formalities → Physiotherapist.
For Andhra Pradesh, the latest Dr. NTR UHS competent-authority route used Intermediate science marks + counselling.
ImportantDo not join an unrecognised “physiotherapy” certificate and assume it makes you a Physiotherapist. Check the exact BPT qualification, university affiliation, hospital exposure and current recognition/registration requirements before admission.
CIRCLE 3 · SPECIALITIES & JOBS

Where can a physiotherapist work?

Physiotherapy is much broader than sports injuries. Different patients need very different rehabilitation.

Orthopaedic / Musculoskeletal
Back pain, neck pain, joint problems, fractures and post-surgery rehabilitation.
Work: movement assessment, exercise, mobility, strength, posture and functional recovery.
Back / NeckJointsPost-surgery
Sports Physiotherapy
Help athletes prevent and recover from injuries. Work can involve return-to-sport testing, strength, mobility, taping, rehabilitation and collaboration with coaches/strength staff.
Reality: getting into elite sports teams is competitive; general BPT does not automatically lead to IPL or national-team jobs.
Neurological Physiotherapy
Help people regain movement and independence after stroke, spinal-cord injury, traumatic brain injury, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis and other neurological conditions.
Cardiorespiratory
Work with people recovering from heart/lung illness, surgery or intensive care. Treatment can include breathing exercises, mobilisation, endurance and pulmonary/cardiac rehabilitation.
Paediatric Physiotherapy
Work with children who have developmental, neurological, orthopaedic or movement difficulties. Family communication and playful, age-appropriate therapy are important.
Geriatric Physiotherapy
Help older adults maintain strength, balance, walking ability and independence, including fall prevention and rehabilitation after illness or surgery.
Women's Health / Pelvic Health
Specialised rehabilitation related to pregnancy, postpartum recovery, pelvic-floor dysfunction and other women's-health conditions. Additional training and careful professional practice are important.
ICU / Hospital Rehabilitation
Help hospitalised patients begin safe movement after major illness, surgery or prolonged bed rest. This can involve medically complex patients and close teamwork with doctors and nurses.
Community / Home Care
Treat patients at home or in community settings — especially older adults, neurological patients and people who cannot travel easily to clinics.
Ergonomics / Workplace
Help organisations reduce musculoskeletal problems through posture, work-station design, movement education and injury-prevention programmes.
Rehabilitation Technology
Physiotherapists increasingly work alongside prosthetics/orthotics, gait labs, robotics, wearable sensors and digital-rehabilitation systems.
Physiotherapist vs Doctor
Physiotherapist: specialises in movement, function, exercise and rehabilitation.
Medical Doctor: follows MBBS and medical-specialty training to diagnose/treat disease using the medical scope of practice.
Physiotherapists are healthcare professionals, but BPT is not MBBS.
Physiotherapist vs Occupational Therapist
Physiotherapy: focuses strongly on movement, mobility, strength, pain and physical function.
Occupational Therapy: focuses strongly on helping people perform meaningful daily activities — dressing, school, work, self-care and participation — using activity, adaptation and rehabilitation.
The professions often work together.
Physiotherapist vs Fitness Trainer
Physiotherapist: clinically trained to assess and rehabilitate patients with injury, disease or functional problems.
Fitness Trainer: primarily helps generally healthy clients improve exercise and fitness.
A gym certification is not a substitute for BPT clinical training.
Who hires
Hospitals, rehabilitation centres, physiotherapy clinics, sports academies/teams, orthopaedic centres, neurological rehabilitation centres, home-health companies, fitness/wellness organisations, schools/special-needs centres and community programmes.
Private practice
With appropriate experience and current professional/legal requirements, physiotherapists may build their own clinic or home-care practice. Income can improve, but clinical reputation, referrals, patient outcomes, rent, equipment and business skills all matter.

CIRCLE 4

What you may earn

Physiotherapy can become a strong long-term healthcare career, but starting salaries in India are often modest. Experience, specialisation and private practice can improve earnings.

Internship
Often low / institute-dependent
BPT includes compulsory clinical internship. Stipend policies vary greatly between colleges/hospitals, so do not assume it will be paid well.
Starting Physiotherapist
₹15,000 – ₹25,000/month
A realistic early-career range for many clinic/hospital roles. Indeed's 2026 India-wide Physiotherapist average is about ₹19,547/month.
Andhra Pradesh benchmark
≈ ₹16,800/month reported average
Indeed's Andhra Pradesh page reported about ₹16,817/month in January 2026, but it was based on only 2 reported salaries, so treat it cautiously. Current local job listings span much wider ranges.
Experienced / specialised
₹25,000 – ₹50,000+/month
Experience in orthopaedics, neuro, cardio-respiratory, sports, ICU, home care or a strong hospital/clinic can move income higher. Location and employer matter greatly.
Private practice / sports
Highly variable
Established clinicians may earn more through clinic consultations, home visits, rehabilitation packages or sports clients. But patient volume, reputation, referrals and operating costs determine actual profit.
Course cost
BPT tuition varies by college and quota. In the latest Dr. NTR UHS 2025–26 competent-authority prospectus, private-college tuition was to be collected at the Government of Andhra Pradesh fixed rate. The prospectus separately listed BPT special fees of ₹13,500 in Year 1 and ₹8,500 in Years 2–4, plus a ₹5,600 university fee at allotment. Always check the current year's fee order before admission.
Ask at homeBefore paying several lakhs for BPT, compare the college's hospital attachment, patient exposure, faculty, internship quality, total fee and actual graduate jobs. A fancy campus is far less important than strong clinical training.

How to read your four circles

Biology + people + rehabilitation — Physiotherapy may be a strong fit.

Love sports but not patient care — compare Sports Science, Strength & Conditioning or professional coaching.

Want to prescribe medicines / become a medical specialist — compare the MBBS pathway instead.

One extra rule: a good physiotherapist does not simply treat pain — they help the patient regain function and independence.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

You can test whether healthcare rehabilitation suits you long before entering BPT.

1Right now, in school
  • Strengthen Biology and learn basic human anatomy — bones, major muscles, joints, heart, lungs and nervous system
  • Play sport or exercise regularly and notice how movement, balance, strength and flexibility change performance
  • Practise explaining a simple exercise safely and clearly to another person
2After Class 10
  • Choose BiPC with English if BPT is one of your serious options
  • Focus strongly on Biology, Physics and Chemistry because Andhra Pradesh's latest BPT merit process used the science-group marks
  • Visit a physiotherapy department or clinic if possible and observe the difference between orthopaedic, neurological and post-surgery rehabilitation
3Before choosing a BPT college
  • Check whether your target college uses CUET-UG, NEET-UG, its own entrance process or Class 12 merit; for Andhra Pradesh, verify the current Dr. NTR UHS prospectus
  • Compare hospital attachment, daily patient load, internship locations, laboratories, faculty and total 4½-year cost
  • Verify the current professional-recognition and registration framework before admission and again when graduating
Best first testChoose a simple movement such as squatting or walking upstairs. Learn which major joints and muscles are involved, then explain how weakness, pain or limited range of motion could make that movement difficult. If connecting anatomy → movement → recovery genuinely interests you, Physiotherapy may suit you.
