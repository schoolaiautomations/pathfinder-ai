Subjects & skills

What to focus on in school, and which road to take after Class 10.

1Biology
You study plants, soil and living things — the core of farming science.
2Physical Science
Chemistry helps you understand fertilisers, pesticides and soil nutrients.
3Social Studies
Geography and civics help you understand rural land, policies and farming communities.
After Class 10
After Class 10, choose Intermediate BiPC (Biology, Physics, Chemistry). This leads to a B.Sc Agriculture degree, which is the main entry route. Alternatively, choose MPC if you prefer Agricultural Engineering as a future option.

CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

This is the circle almost nobody checks — and it decides most careers.

Who hires
Andhra Pradesh government (Department of Agriculture), NABARD, State Farm Corporation, private agri companies like ITC and UPL, NGOs working in rural areas.
How often
APPSC Agriculture Officer exams come every 2 to 4 years. Junior Assistant posts come more often.
How many
Thousands apply for a few hundred seats. Competition is very high for senior posts.
CarefulKeep a backup plan ready. Apply for junior-level posts first to gain experience and government entry.
Your Plan B
Pick one that reuses the same preparation, so no year is wasted:
Agricultural scientist (ICAR)Bank officer (Agri loan department)Agri input sales officer (private)Horticulture officer (state govt)Rural development officer

The cost before that
Intermediate (2 years) plus B.Sc Agriculture (4 years) = 6 non-earning years. Government college fees are Rs 20,000 to 50,000 per year. Private colleges cost much more. Total family cost roughly Rs 3 to 10 lakhs.
Ask at homeCan my family manage the years before I start earning? If you are the only earner-to-be, you need a plan that lets you earn while you prepare.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, starting today, not after results.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you are choosing this career for the wrong reasons. Talk to your counsellor.

Write a deadline today: “If I am not selected by the age of ___, I move to my Plan B.” The deadline is what protects you.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

If the four circles came out right, this is your road. Nothing here needs money or coaching.

1Right now, in school
  • Score well in Biology and Physical Science this year
  • Visit one farm and ask the farmer about his daily problems
  • Search online for what EAMCET Agriculture exam looks like
2After Class 10
  • Join Intermediate BiPC stream in a good junior college
  • Start reading basic agriculture books alongside your syllabus
  • Attend any agriculture awareness camps or Krishi Melas nearby
3After Intermediate or diploma
  • Clear EAMCET and join a government agriculture college
  • Do internships at farms or Krishi Vigyan Kendras during holidays
  • Begin preparing for APPSC Agriculture Officer exam from second year of degree
RememberYou are in school. Nothing is decided yet — and that is your advantage. Every year you start early is a year you will not have to spend waiting later.
