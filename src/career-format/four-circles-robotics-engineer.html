Robotics Engineer

Robotics Engineers build machines that can sense, decide and act. A robot may combine mechanical parts, motors, electronics, sensors, cameras, control systems and software. In the AI era, robotics is expanding beyond factory arms into warehouses, drones, agriculture, healthcare, defence, autonomous vehicles and service robots.

CIRCLE 1

Do you really want Robotics Engineering?

Liking robots is a good start. Building reliable robots means solving mechanical, electrical and software problems repeatedly until everything works together.

Do you enjoy understanding how machines, motors, gears and mechanisms move?

Would you enjoy programming a machine and debugging it when it does not behave as expected?

Are Maths and Physics subjects you are willing to become strong in?

Do sensors, cameras, circuits, microcontrollers and electronics interest you?

Can you patiently test the same robot many times and improve one small failure at a time?

Would you enjoy working in a team where mechanical, electronics and software engineers solve one problem together?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & pathway

Robotics is multidisciplinary. You do not need a degree whose title is exactly “Robotics Engineering” to become a Robotics Engineer.

1Mathematics
Robotics uses algebra, geometry, calculus, matrices and probability for motion, control, localisation and AI.
2Physics
Mechanics, forces, electricity, motors and motion help you understand how a robot behaves in the real world.
3Programming
Python and C/C++ are widely useful. Robotics software also uses algorithms, embedded programming, computer vision, AI and tools such as ROS.
After Class 10
Choose MPC — Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. This keeps the main B.Tech/B.E. engineering routes open for Robotics, Mechatronics, Mechanical, Electronics, Electrical and Computer Science.
Best degree routes
Robotics & AutomationMechatronicsMechanicalECEEEECSE / AI

Do not reject a strong Mechanical/ECE/CSE programme just because its degree title does not say “Robotics.” Robotics employers hire from several engineering disciplines.
Andhra Pradesh entrance
AP EAPCET is the main state engineering entrance route for participating Andhra Pradesh engineering colleges. The exact Robotics / Mechatronics / Automation branches available change by college and counselling year, so check the current seat matrix.
National route
JEE Main is used for NITs, IIITs and other participating institutes; JEE Advanced is used for IIT admission. You can study Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science or related engineering and specialise in robotics through electives and projects.
Diploma route
Class 10 → Diploma in Mechanical / Mechatronics / ECE / EEE / related engineering → AP ECET → lateral-entry B.Tech where eligible.
Higher study
M.Tech/MS can help for advanced Robotics, Control Systems, Autonomous Systems, Mechatronics, AI, Computer Vision or Embedded Systems. GATE / AP PGECET or institute-specific admissions may be relevant. A Master's is useful, but not compulsory for every robotics job.
You must clear
Class 10 → MPC → AP EAPCET / JEE / college entrance as applicable → B.Tech/B.E. in Robotics, Mechatronics, Mechanical, ECE, EEE, CSE or related field → robotics projects + internship → Robotics Engineer.
ImportantDo not choose a weak college only because it has a flashy “Robotics & AI” branch name. Compare laboratories, faculty, coding culture, electronics/mechanical facilities, student robotics teams, internships and placements.
CIRCLE 3 · ROBOTICS JOBS

What kind of Robotics Engineer could you become?

A robot needs mechanics, electronics, control and software. Different engineers specialise in different layers.

Robotics Software Engineer
Write software that makes robots move, plan and perform tasks. Common skills include C++, Python, Linux, ROS, algorithms and simulation.
Mechanical Robotics Engineer
Design robot frames, joints, grippers, gearboxes, linkages and mechanisms using CAD, mechanics and manufacturing knowledge.
Embedded / Electronics Engineer
Work with sensors, motor drivers, microcontrollers, PCBs, power electronics and real-time embedded systems.
Control Systems Engineer
Make robots move accurately and remain stable using feedback control, motor control, dynamics and trajectory tracking.
Computer Vision Engineer
Help robots understand cameras and images — object detection, inspection, tracking, depth and pose estimation.
Perception / AI Engineer
Combine camera, LiDAR, radar and other sensor data so a robot can understand its surroundings.
Autonomous Navigation Engineer
Build localisation, mapping, obstacle avoidance and path planning for mobile robots, drones and autonomous vehicles.
SLAMMappingPlanning
Industrial Robotics Engineer
Program and integrate robotic arms for welding, painting, assembly, packaging, machine tending and material handling.
Automation Engineer
Integrate robots with PLCs, conveyors, sensors, machine vision and production systems.
Robotics Integration Engineer
Combine robots, end-effectors, cameras, safety systems and software into a complete working solution for a customer.
Drone / UAV Engineer
Work on flight control, autonomy, payloads, mapping and navigation for agriculture, surveying, inspection and logistics.
Medical Robotics Engineer
Develop surgical, rehabilitation, assistive and hospital robotics. Advanced roles may require specialised postgraduate study.
Agricultural Robotics Engineer
Build robots, autonomous vehicles, drones and machine-vision systems for seeding, spraying, crop monitoring, weeding and harvesting.
Robotics Research Engineer
Develop new methods for manipulation, locomotion, perception, human–robot interaction and autonomy. Advanced R&D roles often value M.Tech/MS/PhD-level research.
Robotics vs Mechatronics
Mechatronics: broader combination of mechanical + electronics + control + software for intelligent machines.
Robotics: focuses specifically on machines that sense, move and perform tasks with varying autonomy.
Mechatronics is an excellent route into robotics.
Robotics vs AI
AI Engineer: may build software-only intelligent systems.
Robotics Engineer: makes intelligence work in the physical world, where motors, friction, sensor noise, safety and hardware failures matter.
Where you can work
Industrial automation, automotive, warehouses/logistics, drones, defence/aerospace, electronics manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, research labs, startups, machine vision, autonomous vehicles and smart factories.
AI-era reality
AI makes robots better at perception, language and decision-making, but it does not remove the need for mechanical design, electronics, controls, safety and systems integration.
What employers value
ProjectsC++ / PythonROSCADElectronicsControlsGit / Linux
Your Plan B
Robotics skills transfer into:
Mechanical DesignEmbedded SystemsAutomationSoftwareAI / Computer VisionManufacturing
Career reality“I built one Arduino robot” is a good beginning, not job readiness. Employers look for reliable hardware, clean code, controls, debugging, documentation and systems that work repeatedly.
CIRCLE 4

What you may earn

Robotics salary varies widely because the title covers mechanical, automation, embedded, software, computer-vision and R&D roles.

Intern / trainee
₹8,000 – ₹20,000/month
Internships may involve CAD, assembly, testing, PLC/automation, embedded systems or coding.
Starting Robotics Engineer
₹20,000 – ₹35,000/month
A broad practical range for many entry-level Indian robotics and automation jobs. Software-heavy or specialised roles can start higher.
India benchmark
≈ ₹23,440/month
Indeed's February 2026 India-wide Robotics Engineer benchmark was about ₹23,440/month, based on only 13 reported salaries, so treat it as a rough reference.
Experienced engineer
₹35,000 – ₹70,000+/month
Industrial robots, controls, ROS, embedded systems, computer vision and autonomous navigation can raise earning potential.
Advanced software / R&D
₹60,000 – ₹1,50,000+/month
Senior robotics software, perception, autonomous-systems and R&D roles can pay more, but skill expectations are much higher.
Course cost
Engineering fees vary enormously. Government/state engineering seats can be much cheaper than high-fee private Robotics programmes. Do not assume expensive = better. Compare labs, projects and placements.
Money warningDo not take a very large loan only for a “Robotics & AI” course name. A strong core engineering degree plus robotics projects can be a safer and equally powerful route.

How to read your four circles

Machines + coding + electronics — Robotics Engineering may be a strong fit.

Love machines but dislike coding — compare Mechanical / Manufacturing Engineering.

Love AI/coding but dislike hardware — compare Software Engineering / AI & Machine Learning.

One extra rule: a robot is a system. The best robotics engineers learn one part deeply but understand how all the parts connect.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

You do not need an expensive robot kit to test whether this career fits you.

1Right now, in school
  • Strengthen Maths and Physics
  • Start basic Python and later C/C++
  • Build simple projects using Arduino/ESP32, motors and sensors if available
2After Class 10
  • Choose MPC
  • Prepare for AP EAPCET / JEE according to the colleges you target
  • Compare Robotics, Mechatronics, Mechanical, ECE, EEE and CSE — do not focus only on the branch name
3During engineering
  • Join a robotics club and build real projects every year
  • Learn Linux, Git, C++/Python, ROS, sensors, control and basic CAD
  • Get internships in automation, embedded systems, manufacturing, drones, autonomous systems or robotics startups
Best first testBuild a small robot that must do something measurable — for example follow a line, avoid obstacles or move to a target. Then make it work 10 times in a row, not just once. If you enjoy finding and fixing every failure, Robotics Engineering may genuinely suit you.
