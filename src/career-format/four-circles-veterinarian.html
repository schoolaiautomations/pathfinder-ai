Veterinarian / Veterinary Doctor

Veterinarians diagnose, treat and prevent disease in animals. The career is much broader than treating pets — veterinarians work with cattle, buffaloes, poultry, sheep, goats, horses, wildlife, farms, food safety, public health, research and government animal-husbandry services. It combines Biology, medicine, surgery, animal handling and responsibility.

CIRCLE 1

Do you really want veterinary medicine?

Loving animals is important, but veterinary work also includes blood, injuries, difficult decisions, frightened animals and anxious owners.

Do you enjoy Biology and want to understand how animal bodies, diseases and medicines work?

Can you stay calm around injured, sick, frightened or aggressive animals?

Would you be willing to treat farm animals in villages or field conditions, not only dogs and cats in a modern clinic?

Can you handle blood, wounds, injections, surgery and animal births professionally?

Can you communicate sensitively with owners or farmers when an animal is seriously ill or may not survive?

Would you still choose this career knowing the B.V.Sc. & A.H. course takes about 5½ years including internship?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & pathway

For Andhra Pradesh students, BiPC is the standard school route. The entrance exam depends on which seat you are targeting.

1Biology
The most important foundation. Veterinary study later includes anatomy, physiology, microbiology, pathology, medicine, surgery, reproduction and animal nutrition.
2Chemistry
Important for biochemistry, pharmacology, medicines, nutrition and understanding disease processes.
3Physics
Part of the Class 12 eligibility and entrance preparation. It also supports imaging, equipment and scientific understanding.
After Class 10
Intermediate BiPC — Biology, Physics and Chemistry — with English. This is the standard pathway into B.V.Sc. & A.H. (Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry).
Andhra Pradesh entrance
AP EAPCET — the current 2026 Agriculture & Pharmacy stream includes B.V.Sc. & A.H. for Andhra Pradesh admissions. Candidates seeking this course appear for Biology, Physics and Chemistry in AP EAPCET.
For Andhra Pradesh state-quota seats, always check the current SVVU/AP EAPCET admission brochure for that year.
National entrance
NEET-UG — Veterinary Council of India uses the NEET-UG merit list for the 15% All India Quota seats in recognised veterinary colleges. Some institutions, such as ICAR-IVRI, also admit their B.V.Sc. & A.H. students using NEET-UG merit.

You do not necessarily need both exams for every seat. Choose the exam based on the colleges/quota you want.
The degree
B.V.Sc. & A.H. — 5½ years. Sri Venkateswara Veterinary University's regulations currently specify about 4½ years of professional study + 1 year compulsory rotating internship.
What you study
AnatomyPhysiologyAnimal NutritionMicrobiologyPathologyPharmacologyMedicineSurgeryGynaecology
Registration
Veterinary practice is a regulated profession. After completing the recognised B.V.Sc. & A.H. requirements and internship, graduates must complete the applicable State Veterinary Council / Veterinary Council of India registration process to practise professionally.
Higher study
M.V.Sc. allows specialisation in areas such as Veterinary Medicine, Surgery & Radiology, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, Pathology, Microbiology, Public Health, Animal Nutrition, Genetics & Breeding, Poultry Science and other disciplines. A PhD can lead further into research and academia.
You must clear
Class 10 → Intermediate BiPC + English → AP EAPCET for AP state route and/or NEET-UG for VCI All India / other NEET-based routes → B.V.Sc. & A.H. (5½ years including internship) → professional registration → Veterinarian / Veterinary Doctor.
ImportantDo not confuse a veterinary diploma or animal-health assistant course with B.V.Sc. & A.H. Supporting animal-health roles are valuable, but they do not make someone a registered Veterinary Doctor.
CIRCLE 3 · CAREER AREAS

What kind of veterinarian could you become?

Veterinary medicine covers individual animals, entire farms, wildlife populations, food safety and research.

Pet / Small-Animal Vet
Dogs, cats and companion animals. Work includes vaccination, illness diagnosis, emergency care, surgery, preventive medicine and owner counselling.
DogsCatsPet Clinics
Livestock Veterinarian
Cattle, buffaloes, sheep and goats. Treat disease, assist difficult births, improve herd health, control infections and support farmers.
Andhra Pradesh relevance: dairy and livestock services create significant field demand.
Poultry Veterinarian
Work with poultry farms on flock health, vaccination, disease prevention, biosecurity, nutrition and production. Large poultry operations employ veterinarians for preventive health and disease-control programmes.
Veterinary Surgeon
Perform soft-tissue, orthopaedic and emergency procedures after appropriate veterinary training and experience. Advanced specialists may complete M.V.Sc. or further clinical training.
Veterinary Medicine
Diagnose and manage internal diseases involving digestive, respiratory, infectious, metabolic and other systems across animal species.
Reproduction / Gynaecology
Animal breeding, pregnancy care, infertility, artificial insemination support, obstetrics and reproductive health — especially important in livestock and dairy systems.
Wildlife Veterinarian
Work with zoos, wildlife-rescue centres, conservation projects and forest/wildlife departments. Opportunities are fewer and highly competitive, often requiring additional experience or postgraduate training.
Veterinary Public Health
Protect both animal and human health. Work can involve zoonotic diseases, food-borne disease, meat/milk hygiene, epidemiology, disease surveillance and One Health programmes.
Veterinary Pathology / Diagnostics
Diagnose disease through laboratory tests, tissue examination, microbiology, pathology and post-mortem investigation. Important in hospitals, labs, research and disease-surveillance systems.
Animal Nutrition
Develop feeding programmes for livestock, poultry, pets and other animals. Jobs exist in feed companies, farms, research institutes and animal-health businesses.
Government Veterinary Officer
State animal-husbandry departments recruit veterinarians for field hospitals, disease control, livestock programmes and public services. Recruitment method and pay depend on the current state notification.
Research Scientist
Veterinary graduates can enter research in animal diseases, vaccines, genetics, reproduction, nutrition, public health and biotechnology through M.V.Sc./PhD and institutes such as ICAR veterinary research centres.
Animal-Health Industry
Veterinary pharmaceutical, vaccine, feed, diagnostics and animal-health companies hire veterinarians for technical services, product development, clinical support, sales and regulatory roles.
Veterinarian vs MBBS Doctor
Veterinarian: medically trained to diagnose and treat animals through B.V.Sc. & A.H.
Human Doctor: follows MBBS and treats people.
Both are medical professions, but their degrees, licensing and patients are completely different.
Veterinarian vs Animal Husbandry Assistant
Veterinarian: completes the regulated B.V.Sc. & A.H. professional degree and registration.
Animal-health / husbandry support staff: may hold diploma or vocational qualifications and work under a different scope of practice.
Do not treat the titles as equivalent.
Pet clinic vs field work
Pet practice: usually clinic-based, owner-facing and increasingly specialised.
Livestock/government work: can involve village visits, farms, animal outbreaks, reproductive work and emergency calls under less controlled conditions.
Try to understand both before choosing the career.
Where you can work
Government veterinary hospitals, private pet hospitals, dairy/livestock farms, poultry companies, zoos/wildlife programmes, diagnostic laboratories, universities, ICAR institutes, veterinary pharma/vaccine companies, feed companies and NGOs.
Your Plan B
Veterinary training can also lead into:

RealityThis career is not just “playing with animals.” Veterinarians make medical decisions, restrain frightened animals, handle emergencies, work with farmers/owners and sometimes deal with animal death and euthanasia.
CIRCLE 4

What you may earn

Veterinary income varies significantly between government service, livestock/poultry companies, pet practice, research and private clinics.

Early-career private vet
₹20,000 – ₹40,000/month
A broad starting range for many private clinics, farms and animal-health roles. City, employer, species and duty pattern matter greatly.
India benchmark
≈ ₹30,000/month average
Indeed's 2026 India-wide veterinarian data is around ₹30,000/month. The sample is small, so use it only as a rough market reference.
Andhra Pradesh benchmark
≈ ₹33,000/month reported average
Indeed's latest Andhra Pradesh figure is around ₹33,184/month, but it is based on only a few reported salaries, so it should not be treated as a guaranteed state salary.
Experienced / specialised
₹40,000 – ₹80,000+/month
Experienced pet clinicians, surgeons, poultry/livestock veterinarians, specialists and industry veterinarians can earn more depending on skill and employer.
Government Veterinary Officer
Government pay depends on the specific state/central recruitment notification and pay scale. It can include basic pay and applicable allowances, but do not use an old Veterinary Officer notification as a permanent salary promise.
Own clinic / hospital
Highly variable
A successful pet clinic can earn well, but equipment, rent, staff, medicines, diagnostics and emergency coverage are expensive. Revenue is not the same as profit.
Study length & cost
B.V.Sc. & A.H. takes about 5½ years, so consider the full cost of tuition, hostel/living expenses, books, instruments and the longer period before full-time earning. Government veterinary colleges are generally much more affordable than high-fee private options.
Ask at homeBecause this is a long professional course, compare total 5½-year cost, recognised status, clinical caseload, livestock exposure, teaching hospital and internship quality before choosing a college. Do not choose an unrecognised programme simply because admission is easier.

How to read your four circles

Biology + animals + medical responsibility — Veterinary medicine may be a strong fit.

Love animals but dislike blood / medicine — explore Zoology, Wildlife Biology, Animal Science or pet-industry careers.

Want only dogs and cats — remember that veterinary education includes many animal species and livestock systems before you specialise.

One extra rule: loving animals makes you care. Veterinary training teaches you how to care for them medically.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

You can test your interest before committing to a 5½-year medical degree.

1Right now, in school
  • Build strong Biology fundamentals, especially anatomy, cells, disease and reproduction
  • Learn about livestock and poultry as well as pets — not only dogs and cats
  • If possible, visit a veterinary hospital, dairy farm or animal-health camp with an adult and observe the real work
2After Class 10
  • Choose BiPC with English
  • For Andhra Pradesh state veterinary seats, prepare for the current AP EAPCET Biology-Physics-Chemistry route
  • If you want the VCI 15% All India Quota or NEET-based veterinary institutions, prepare for NEET-UG as well
3Before choosing college
  • Verify that the college/degree is recognised for veterinary registration
  • Compare teaching hospital, livestock farms, surgery exposure, diagnostic labs and internship quality
  • Check the latest admission brochure because entrance rules, seat matrices and cut-offs change each year
Best first testSpend a day observing a real veterinarian if possible — ideally one pet clinic and one livestock/field setting. Notice whether you are still interested when the work involves disease, injections, restraint, wounds, animal owners/farmers and uncertainty — not only healthy animals.
