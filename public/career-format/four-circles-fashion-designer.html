CIRCLE 1

Do you really want it?

Many students want the glamour of fashion, not the daily work behind it. Tap your honest answer.

Can you spend hours measuring, cutting, pinning and correcting one garment?

Can you redo a design after a client, teacher or production team rejects your first idea?

Do you enjoy learning how fabric falls, stretches, shrinks and behaves — not only drawing clothes?

Can you work under tight deadlines before weddings, launches, shoots or seasonal collections?

Can you accept blunt criticism about your taste and still improve the work without taking it personally?

Are you willing to start as an assistant, intern or junior designer before you get creative control?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & skills

What to focus on in school, and which road to take after Class 10.

1Drawing & Art
Fashion starts with observation, proportion, colour and visual ideas. You do not need to be a perfect artist, but regular sketching builds design thinking.
2Maths
Measurements, pattern dimensions, fabric use, sizing and costing all need accurate basic maths.
3English
Entrance tests, portfolios, trend research, software, design briefs and many professional interviews use English.
After Class 10
Intermediate / Class 12 in any recognized stream — MPC, BiPC, CEC or Humanities all work. After Class 12, you can join B.Des Fashion Design or a related fashion course. If targeting NIFT, you must clear the NIFT entrance process; other colleges may use their own entrance test or Class 12 merit.
Start these habits
Sketch clothes weeklyLearn basic hand stitchingStudy fabrics in shopsMake colour moodboardsAlter old clothesFollow fashion shows critically
THE LONGER ROAD — QUALIFICATIONS YOU WILL NEED LATER
Entrance exams
For NIFT B.Des Fashion Design: GAT + CAT → Situation Test if shortlisted. Other fashion/design colleges may conduct their own aptitude or design entrance test, interview, portfolio review, or admit through Class 12 merit.
B.Des / B.Sc / B.Voc Fashion Design
Usually Class 12 pass; admission may be by institute entrance exam, portfolio, interview or merit. Course length is commonly 3–4 years depending on the programme.
Diploma in Fashion Design
Available after Class 10 or Class 12 at different institutes. Usually 1–2 years. Check recognition, practical sewing/pattern training and placement record before paying fees.
You must clear
Class 12 in any stream → NIFT entrance / college admission → B.Des Fashion Design or related course → portfolio + garment-making skills → internship → junior / assistant fashion designer.
Do firstDo not choose Fashion Design only because you like clothes, shopping or Instagram. Before paying for a course, make one simple wearable garment yourself and see whether you enjoy the measuring, stitching, fitting and correction process.
CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

This is the circle almost nobody checks — and it decides most careers.

Who hires
Fashion labels, boutiques, ethnic and bridal-wear studios, apparel manufacturers, export houses, retail brands, e-commerce companies and costume/styling teams. Andhra Pradesh has apparel manufacturing activity around Visakhapatnam; Hyderabad is the nearest larger fashion and design job market.
How often
Private openings appear throughout the year, but pure “Fashion Designer” roles are fewer than students expect. Many graduates begin as assistant designers, merchandisers, stylists, sample coordinators, fashion consultants or production support.
How many
Competition is high because portfolios are compared directly. A degree alone does not guarantee a design job; employers look for garment construction, fabric knowledge, trend research, software skills, internships and proof that you can take a design from sketch to finished sample.
CarefulIf you refuse to learn stitching, patterns, fittings, sourcing and production, you may like fashion as a hobby but not Fashion Design as a career.
Your Plan B
Pick one that reuses the same preparation, so no year is wasted:
Apparel merchandiserTextile designerFashion stylistVisual merchandiserPattern / garment technician
CIRCLE 4

What you will earn

And what it costs before the first salary arrives.

Starting (0–2 years)
Rs 15,000 – 30,000
Assistant or junior designer. Smaller studios can be lower; strong portfolios and metro roles can be higher.
Mid-level (3–6 years)
Rs 30,000 – 70,000
Designer handling collections, clients, sampling, sourcing or a category independently.
Senior (7+ years)
Rs 60,000 – 1,50,000+
Senior designer, design lead, brand role or established boutique. Own-label income can be much lower or much higher because business risk is real.
The cost before that
A 1-year diploma can cost around Rs 80,000–1,50,000 at some recognised institutes, while a 4-year NIFT B.Des is a major investment: the published 2026 regular academic fee schedule totals about Rs 14.09 lakh across eight semesters before hostel, living, fabrics, tools and project costs. Private design schools can cost more.
Ask at homeCan my family afford both tuition and the hidden material costs — fabrics, trims, printing, sewing, portfolio shoots, travel and possibly living in Hyderabad or another city? Do not judge this career only by the course fee on the brochure.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, starting today, not after results.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you are choosing this career for the wrong reasons. Talk to your counsellor.

Write a deadline today: “If I am not progressing toward a real portfolio, internship or job by the age of ___, I move to my Plan B.” The deadline is what protects you.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

If the four circles came out right, this is your road. Start with skill tests before spending money on coaching.

1Right now, in school
  • Sketch 20 different outfits from real people, not only copied Pinterest images
  • Learn basic hand stitches and alter one old shirt, kurta or dress at home
  • Visit a fabric shop and learn the names and feel of at least 10 common fabrics
2After Class 10
  • Choose any Intermediate stream you can score well in — do not force MPC or BiPC only for Fashion Design
  • If targeting NIFT, prepare for GAT + CAT and later the Situation Test if shortlisted
  • Learn basic sewing, pattern making, Adobe Illustrator or another fashion drawing tool and build a small portfolio
3After Class 12
  • Apply to NIFT Hyderabad and other colleges only after comparing course, fees, workshops, internships and placement outcomes
  • Build a portfolio with 8–12 strong projects showing process: research → sketch → fabric → pattern → sample → final garment
  • Take internships with boutiques, apparel manufacturers, export houses or fashion brands in Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Hyderabad or larger fashion hubs
RememberYou do not need expensive clothes to test this career. A sketchbook, needle, thread, old fabric and the discipline to finish real garments will tell you more than months of scrolling fashion content.

This is guidance, not an official admission or salary notice. Course eligibility, entrance pattern, fees, vacancies and pay change over time — confirm the latest details on the official institute or employer website before applying or paying any fee.
