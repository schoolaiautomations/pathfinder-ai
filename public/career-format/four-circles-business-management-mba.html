CIRCLE 1

Do you really want it?

Many students want the title “manager” — not the targets, numbers, meetings and responsibility behind it. Tap your honest answer.

Do you enjoy organising people, tasks and deadlines instead of only doing your own individual work?

Can you speak clearly, present an idea and convince people without getting uncomfortable?

Are you comfortable working with numbers, percentages, budgets, graphs and spreadsheets?

Can you handle targets, competition and being responsible when a project or team does not perform well?

Are you willing to begin as an analyst, trainee, sales executive or coordinator instead of becoming a senior manager immediately after MBA?

Do you like understanding why customers buy, why companies succeed, how teams work and how business decisions are made?

CIRCLE 2

Subjects & skills

What to focus on in school, and the simple road from Class 10 to an MBA.

1English
Managers write emails, present ideas, negotiate, interview people and explain decisions. Strong reading, speaking and writing matter in both MBA entrance exams and jobs.
2Maths & Data
Percentages, profit and loss, ratios, averages, graphs, statistics and logical reasoning appear in entrance exams and everyday business decisions.
3Business Awareness
Learn basic economics, companies, markets, banking, technology and current affairs. You should be curious about how organisations actually work.
After Class 10
Intermediate CEC / Commerce is the most directly related route, but MPC, BiPC, Humanities or any recognized stream also works. After Class 12, first complete a bachelor’s degree such as BBA, B.Com, BA, B.Sc or B.Tech. MBA is normally done after graduation.

THE LONGER ROAD — QUALIFICATIONS YOU WILL NEED LATER
Bachelor's degree
You can enter MBA from many undergraduate backgrounds. BBA/B.Com are directly related, but engineering, science, arts and other recognised degrees are also common. Your target MBA college decides its exact graduation eligibility.
Entrance exams
There is no single entrance exam for every MBA college.
CAT — IIMs, including IIM Visakhapatnam, and many other leading B-schools.
AP ICET — MBA admissions in participating colleges across Andhra Pradesh.
XAT / CMAT / MAT — used by different management institutes across India.
You do not need to write all of them. First shortlist colleges, then prepare for the exams they accept.
After the exam
Depending on the institute, a good entrance score may be followed by Personal Interview, Group Discussion and/or Written Ability Test. Colleges may also consider Class 10, Class 12, graduation marks, work experience and profile.
MBA specialisations
Common choices include Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations, Business Analytics, Strategy and General Management. Do not choose a specialisation only because somebody says it has the “highest salary.” Match it to your skills and target job.
Work experience
Many regular two-year MBA programmes accept fresh graduates, so work experience is not always compulsory. But working for 1–3 years before MBA can help you understand business better and make a more informed specialisation choice.
Alternative route
Some universities also offer a 5-year Integrated BBA + MBA after Class 12. This can be useful, but it is not required. Compare flexibility, fees and placement quality before committing for five years.
You must clear
Class 12 in any stream → bachelor’s degree → MBA entrance exam (CAT / AP ICET / XAT / CMAT / MAT depending on college) → GD/WAT/PI if required → MBA / PGDM → internship + placement → business role.
RememberMBA does not automatically make you a manager. Your first role may be Management Trainee, Analyst, Sales Executive, Associate, HR Executive or Operations Coordinator. Management responsibility comes with performance and experience.
CIRCLE 3 · THE RISK

How often are the jobs?

Business jobs are everywhere — but the quality of the MBA college can change the type of opportunity you get.

Who hires
Banks, consulting firms, FMCG companies, IT and technology firms, manufacturing, pharma, hospitals, retail, logistics, e-commerce, startups, financial services and almost every large organisation with sales, finance, HR or operations teams.
Typical first roles
Management Trainee, Business Analyst, Sales / Marketing Executive, Relationship Manager, HR Executive, Recruiter, Operations Analyst, Supply Chain Associate, Financial Analyst, Product / Project Associate and Business Development Executive.
How often
There are many business roles, but high-paying campus jobs are much more competitive. College reputation, internships, communication, analytical ability, previous work experience and the economic hiring cycle all affect placement outcomes.
The biggest risk
Paying a very high MBA fee at a college with weak placements. Two colleges can both award an MBA but produce completely different job outcomes. Check actual placement reports, median salary, roles offered, internship quality and how many students were really placed.
CarefulDo not join an MBA simply because you are unsure what to do after graduation. It is often smarter to work for one or two years, understand what role you like, then choose the right MBA and specialisation.
Your Plan B
If MBA admission or ROI is not right yet, start working after your bachelor’s degree and build experience:
Business analystSales executiveOperations coordinatorHR recruiterBanking / finance roleEntrepreneurship
CIRCLE 4

What you will earn

MBA salaries vary more by college, role and previous experience than by the three letters “MBA.”

Starting — local / average college
Rs 25,000 – 50,000/month
Common for entry-level sales, HR, operations, banking, analyst and management-trainee roles. Some offers can be lower or higher.
Strong B-school / campus role
Rs 50,000 – 1,50,000+/month
Consulting, analytics, finance, product, management trainee and corporate roles can enter this range. CTC is not the same as monthly take-home salary.
Mid-career (5–10 years)
Rs 1,00,000 – 3,00,000+/month
Managers and specialists in strong industries can earn more, but progression depends on performance, function, city and company.
Top-tier exception
Top IIMs and elite B-schools can produce much higher packages, but these are not representative of every MBA. Use the median salary of the exact college you are considering, not celebrity packages or the single highest offer.
The cost before that
MBA fees vary enormously. As an Andhra Pradesh benchmark, IIM Visakhapatnam's PGP (MBA) 2026–28 academic fee totals about Rs 23.59 lakh, before the refundable caution deposit and term advances for mess/other charges. IIM Bangalore lists Rs 26 lakh for its 2026–28 two-year PGP. Many state-university and AP ICET colleges cost much less, so compare the exact college fee before deciding.
Ask at homeIf an MBA costs Rs 15–25+ lakh, what is the college's median placement salary — not highest package? How much loan will we need? What will the EMI be? A famous-sounding MBA with poor placement ROI can become a financial burden.

How to read your four circles

All four right — go ahead with full commitment, but choose the college and specialisation carefully.

Any one wrong — stop and think twice before you decide.

Two or more wrong — you may be attracted to the title “MBA” rather than the actual work. Talk to your counsellor.

For MBA, one extra rule matters: college + skills + internships + work experience matter more than simply having the degree.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

What to do from today

You do not need to wait until graduation to test whether business and management suit you.

1Right now, in school
  • Learn percentages, profit/loss, ratios, graphs and basic Excel or Google Sheets
  • Take responsibility for one school activity — budget, team, event, club or small project — and finish it on time
  • Read one simple business story each week and explain: What does this company sell? Who is the customer? How does it make money?
2After Class 10
  • Choose CEC / Commerce if you naturally like business and economics, but remember that any recognised Intermediate stream can lead to MBA
  • Improve English, mental maths, logical reasoning, public speaking, presentations and spreadsheets
  • Try a small real-world project: sell something at a school event, manage a budget, run a student club, survey customers or organise a team
3During bachelor's & after graduation
  • Complete a recognised bachelor’s degree and take internships in sales, finance, HR, operations, analytics or another area that interests you
  • Shortlist MBA colleges first, then prepare for the correct entrance exam — CAT, AP ICET, XAT, CMAT, MAT or another test accepted by your target institutes
  • Before accepting admission, compare total fee, median placement, roles offered, batch size, internship quality, alumni outcomes and loan EMI
Best first testGive yourself Rs 1,000 of imaginary capital and design a tiny business: What will you sell? Who will buy it? What will it cost? What price will you charge? How will you market it? If solving those questions excites you, business management may fit you well.
